Requires a running llama-server exposing `/v1/embeddings` (default `http://127.0.0.1:8081/v1/embeddings`) and environment variables `EMBED_URL`, `EMBED_API_KEY`, `PACK_SIGNING_KEY_HEX` (64 hex chars = 32-byte Ed25519 seed), `PACK_SIGNING_KEY_ID`, and for deltas also `PACK_CONTENT_KEY_HEX`. Example: `python3 services/packs/build_pack.py --src services/packs/general-starter --out /tmp/general-starter --embed-url http://127.0.0.1:8081/v1/embeddings`.
